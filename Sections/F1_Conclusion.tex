\section{Conclusion}
This work shows that particulate composites based on a fully bio-sourceable
 thermoset resin can be successfully optimised with respect to their apparent
 compressive modulus and peak compressive stress by adapting their composition 
 during manufacture.
The multi-objective campaign employing the log noisy expected hypervolume
 improvement acquisition function identified a composition that simultaneously
 improved \(E_{\text{app}}\) and \(\sigma_{\text{max}}\), yielding the single
 non-dominated formulation among the 71 samples evaluated
 (sample 68 exhibiting \(E_{\text{app}}=5,458\)~\unit{\mega\pascal} and 
 \(\sigma_{\text{max}}=165\)~\unit{\mega\pascal}).

In the domain of a sample-constrained single-objective Bayesian optimisation,
 using the log-noisy expected improvement acquisition function,
 a composite based on the fully bio-sourceable resin was obtained which exhibited
  69\% of the apparent compressive modulus of a conventional styrene-based 
  material which had been optimised in the same fashion.
Within matched 24-sample campaigns, the best observed dimethyl-itaconate
 formulation retained 69\% of the apparent compressive modulus of the best 
 observed styrene-diluted analogue.
The multi-objective formulation retained 75\% of that comparator's apparent
modulus.
Although there exist no directly comparable studies concerning these
 differences, the extent to which this bio-sourceable reactive diluent
 has managed to partially substitute this conventional fossil-derived
 monomer benchmark is notable.

The present study optimised compressive properties only; tensile, flexural,
 fracture, thermal, and ageing properties remain to be assessed.
Replicate testing at selected formulations would further quantify
 experimental noise and improve \(\mathcal{GP}\) noise estimation.
Finally, although the resin chemistry is bio-sourceable, full sustainability
 assessment would require lifecycle analysis of monomer production, curing,
 filler sourcing, and end-of-life processing.
Given the interesting functional relationships witnessed within the parameter
 space with respect to predictor \(x_{1}\), which concerns the balance between
 unsaturated polyester and reactive diluent, future work could examine how
 reactive diluents impact the mechanical properties of these
 materials.
An optimisation of the styrene-based analogues in the single-objective setting
 with respect to the objective of peak compressive stress would also be a 
 worthwhile study.
